    📖 Void Edge 2.0 Field Manual

    1. Five-Tier Roster: Each team launches 5 marbles in escalating value: White (1pt), Yellow (2pt), Red (3pt), Green (5pt), and Cyan Heavy Diamond (10pt).
    2. The Gravity Slope & Void: Marbles entering the dashed inner square are pulled inward toward the central black Void pit.
    3. Touch-Rule Scoring: You score points whenever an opponent's marble falls into the Void—or when your own marble knocks an enemy in. Dropping your own untouched marble into the pit awards 0 points!
    4. Sudden Death & Void Cam: Tied after 10 shots? Both teams spawn 10pt Cyan Diamonds for sudden-death knockout rounds. High-value eliminations trigger slow-mo Void Cam replays.
